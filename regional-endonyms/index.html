What do these outlines mean?

This prototype brings together eight selected names. It distinguishes cultural regions from administrative units; these are different kinds of geography. Dashed shapes are deliberately coarse editorial sketches, informed by the linked descriptions. They are not digitized scholarly boundaries, surveyed borders, measures of ethnic composition, or claims to sovereignty. Greenland uses generalized Natural Earth geometry.

Language and territorial identity do not correspond one-to-one. The selected label is one endonym, not a claim of a single language or unanimous identity. Each record states the extent chosen and what it leaves out. Points serve only as label anchors.
