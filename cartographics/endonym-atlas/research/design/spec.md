# Design Specification

The generated `concept.png` is the visual reference. It is a concept only; actual borders and labels are rendered from geographic data.

Use an ivory (#f7f5ee) page, teal-gray (#dce9e6) ocean, sage land, forest (#253d36) ink, and terracotta (#ae593e) selection. Use Georgia for display headings and country labels; use system sans-serif for functional controls. The header is 76 px tall; the toolbar is 70 px tall. The remaining viewport contains a full map and a 330 px detail rail, with a small bottom status strip. On mobile the rail follows the map vertically.

Required copy and components: Endonym Atlas, A World Named From Within, About & Sources, Map Labels, Native Script, Romanized, Pronunciation, Find a country or language, Language, Sources. Start with Japan selected, Japanese / 日本 / Nihon / nee-hohn. Render labels with collision avoidance and increase available detail on zoom. Include native spelling, Latin spelling, approximate English pronunciation, and verified recorded audio in the rail. Source and coverage notices, a country list, and keyboard-accessible map navigation extend the concept to meet the research and accessibility requirements. Exact geography and research-driven wording take precedence over erroneous invented map text in the image.
