The following chart lists [countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states "List of sovereign states") and [dependencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dependent_territory "Dependent territory") along with their [capital cities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_(political) "Capital (political)"), in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language "English language") and non-English [official language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Official_language "Official language")(s).

- In **bold**: [internationally recognized sovereign states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states "List of sovereign states")
  - The 193 [member states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_United_Nations_member_states "List of United Nations member states") of the [United Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations "United Nations") (UN)
  - [Vatican City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vatican_City "Vatican City") (administered by the [Holy See](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_See "Holy See"), a [UN observer state](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_General_Assembly_observers#Non-member_observers "United Nations General Assembly observers")), which is generally recognized as a sovereign state
- In _**bold italics**_: [states with limited recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_states_with_limited_recognition "List of states with limited recognition") and [associated states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_state "Associated state") not members of the United Nations

  - _De facto_ sovereign states with partial international recognition, such as [Palestine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestine "Palestine"), [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo "Kosovo") and [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan "Taiwan")
  - _De facto_ sovereign states lacking general international recognition
  - [Cook Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cook_Islands "Cook Islands") and [Niue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niue "Niue"), two [associated states of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realm_of_New_Zealand "Realm of New Zealand") without UN membership
- In _italics_: non-sovereign territories that are recognized by the UN as part of some member state

  - [Dependent territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_dependent_territories "List of dependent territories")
  - Special territories recognized by international treaty (such as the [special administrative regions of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_administrative_regions_of_China "Special administrative regions of China"))
  - Other territories often regarded as separate geographical territories even though they are integral parts of their mother countries (such as the [overseas departments of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Overseas_departments_and_territories_of_France "Overseas departments and territories of France"))

1. [↑](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_and_their_capitals_in_native_languages#cite_ref-a_13-0 "Jump up")[Jerusalem Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem_Law "Jerusalem Law") states that "Jerusalem, complete and united, is the capital of Israel", and the city serves as the seat of the government, home to the president's residence, government offices, supreme court, and parliament. [United Nations Security Council Resolution 478](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Security_Council_Resolution_478 "United Nations Security Council Resolution 478") declared the Jerusalem Law "null and void" and called on member states to withdraw their diplomatic missions from Jerusalem. The United Nations and nearly all of its member states maintain their embassies in other cities such as [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv "Tel Aviv"), [Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan "Ramat Gan"), and [Herzliya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herzliya "Herzliya"), and do not recognise Jerusalem as the capital;[\[21\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_and_their_capitals_in_native_languages#cite_note-23) however, in May 2018, the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States "United States") and [Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guatemala "Guatemala") moved their official embassies to Jerusalem, and several other UN member states indicated that they were considering doing the same.[\[22\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_and_their_capitals_in_native_languages#cite_note-24) Many countries officially adhere to the proposal that Jerusalem have international status, as called for in the [Partition Plan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Partition_Plan_for_Palestine "United Nations Partition Plan for Palestine") of 1947. The [Palestinian National Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palestinian_National_Authority "Palestinian National Authority") claims East Jerusalem as the capital of a future Palestinian state.[\[23\]](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_countries_and_dependencies_and_their_capitals_in_native_languages#cite_note-25) See [Positions on Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positions_on_Jerusalem "Positions on Jerusalem").

The proclaimed state is currently occupied by Israel and has no effective control over the territories it claims.
