Selected time windows · spacing is not proportional to elapsed years

Origins are places. Journeys are histories.

The five ingredients are a curated sample. Native range, centre of diversity, domestication, and first surviving evidence are different measures. The green marker anchors the origin description; it does not draw a botanical range. Dated markers reveal selected evidence up to the selected time window, not the full distribution of a crop at that time.

Lines connect broad locations for illustration. They are not reconstructed shipping tracks or proof of direct descent from one planting to another. Archaeological observations of cacao are shown without connecting lines. Broad dates are assigned display anchors and identified in each event. Modern coastlines and borders provide geographic context, not historical political boundaries.

Plant movements involved Indigenous knowledge, trade, colonial violence, and coerced labour. These records do not measure those processes comprehensively. The botanical artwork was AI-generated for decoration and is not an identification guide.
